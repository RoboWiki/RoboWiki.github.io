# Parameter semantics

One hard rule on this site: **torque and speed always mean the output side (gearbox included).**

- `A` tier = every field checked against a vendor manual. `B` = public web page, not cross-checked.
  `C` = relayed.
- `—` means *not published*, not *not supported*, and never `0`. Do not fill gaps from similar parts.
- Missing is missing: a blank field is a claim we could not source, not a value of zero.

Example: the DJI M3508 manual quotes 0.16 N·m at the motor side. We publish 3.07 N·m at the
output side (× 19.203208556, protocol value 3591/187) and keep the original number and the
arithmetic in that part's notes.

Ordering always goes by spec and measured data — never by who contributed more.
