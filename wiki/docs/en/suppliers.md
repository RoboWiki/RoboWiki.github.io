    <div class="sup-kicker">Suppliers</div>
    <h1>Who actually makes the part.</h1>
    <p>
      Not part of the parts library. Parts compare <strong>specs</strong>; this page compares
      <strong>who is reliable</strong>: machining quality, whether they deliver on time, price
      tier, whether they can issue an invoice. Mixing the two would inflate the part count and
      turn brand filtering into 145 buttons.
    </p>
    <div class="sup-src">
      <b>Source</b>
      From a community-compiled supplier directory, grouped by the directory's own categories:
      standard parts / misc / machining / fasteners / ESD protection / electronics / sensors /
      bearings — 149 shops, all actually used by the community with feedback left. There is no
      "well-known vendor" channel: no evidence, no listing. Quality / lead time / price / invoice
      are <strong>relayed field reports</strong>, not vendor claims, marked tier <code>B</code>.
      Original signatures (school + name) have been fully de-identified.
    </div>
